Goose Nest Guardian wiring

Every connection for the nest: five grass-feather servos, two distance sensors, two motion sensors, a DHT11, a KY-037 sound sensor, a light sensor and an optional buzzer. The two long lines around the right side are your breadboard's power rails. Where a wire hops over another, they are not connected.

On a phone, scroll the diagram sideways. A dot means two wires are joined.

Before you power it on

  • Don't power the servos from the Arduino. Their red (+) wires go to the separate supply only.
  • Join the grounds. The servo supply's − must connect to an Arduino GND, or the servos will twitch randomly or not move at all.
  • Read the labels on your modules. DHT11 and HC-SR501 boards don't all put their pins in the same order. Match by name (VCC, GND, OUT, S), not by position.
  • Never join 5V straight to GND. Check the two rails with your eyes before plugging in the USB cable.
